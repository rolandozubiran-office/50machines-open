    • What it does

    Hand-wound coils and magnets: a generator you build instead of salvage.

    This part of the tree: A battery, then a magnet, then a wheel-driven alternator. From here power can travel down a wire.

    Model

    A Piggott-style axial-flux alternator: two steel discs with rings of magnets, a cast-resin stator of hand-wound coils between them, a hub and spindle on a mount, a rectifier and battery. Build it exploded-then-assembled. Run: spin speed slider → volts and watts, three-phase; ~500 W at 425 rpm.
