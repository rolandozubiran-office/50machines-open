## What it does

Turns wood chips into a fuel gas that runs an engine.

This part of the tree: Cold food, hot water, cooking gas and engines that run on wood or sunlight.

## Model

A FEMA stratified downdraft gasifier: fuel hopper on a fire tube with air nozzles, grate, ash pit, a cyclone and filter barrel, a carburettor valve feeding a small engine coupled to a generator. Run: light, draw, gas flows, engine starts, generator watts. Strong carbon monoxide safety line: outdoors only.
