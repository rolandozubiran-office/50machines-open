## What it does

Two clay pots and wet sand: evaporation keeps food cold with no power.

This part of the tree: Cold food, hot water, cooking gas and engines that run on wood or sunlight.

## Model

Pot-in-pot coolers on a wooden stand in the shade: an outer clay pot, wet sand between, an inner pot with vegetables, a wet cloth lid, thermometers inside and outside, a water jug. Run: a sped-up hot day; outside temperature swings, inside stays lower; water topped up; shelf life 20 days vs 2 for tomatoes.
