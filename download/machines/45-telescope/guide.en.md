## What it does

Two lenses in a tube for navigation, surveying and the sky.

This part of the tree: Print, time, direction, then messages over wires and through the air, and finally a machine that calculates.

## Model

A refracting telescope on a wooden tripod with an alt-azimuth mount: main tube with objective lens cell, sliding draw tube and eyepiece, a finder; beside it a lens-grinding stand with a tool and abrasive. Run: focus; magnification = f_objective / f_eyepiece (≈ 20×).
