## What it does

Paper, iron-gall ink and cast type copy knowledge fast.

This part of the tree: Print, time, direction, then messages over wires and through the air, and finally a machine that calculates.

## Model

A wooden screw hand press (Gutenberg/common press): frame, screw and bar, platen, carriage with bed and type forme, tympan and frisket, ink balls; beside it a paper vat with mould and deckle and a type case. Run: ink, lay a sheet, run in, pull, run out; a printed sheet joins the pile; 250 sheets per hour by two pressmen (Moxon).
