    • What it does

    Lye from wood ash and fat makes soap. Handwashing cuts diarrhoea by about 30%.

    This part of the tree: Fired clay, boiled water and soap stop most early deaths. Charcoal and lime set up the heat and materials for everything after.

    Model

    A wooden ash hopper (V-trough on legs with a straw-and-gravel filter) dripping lye into a clay pot; the egg-float strength test; a soap kettle over a fire; wooden soap moulds; plus a tippy-tap handwashing station. Run: leach lye, test it, boil with fat until trace, pour into moulds, cut bars.
