## What it does

Carries one water wheel's power to every machine in a workshop.

This part of the tree: A forge, a foundry and a lathe make tools that make tools. Water wheels and gears put a river to work.

## Model

A workshop line shaft on hangers from a beam, driven by a belt from a power source at one end, with flat belts down to a drill press, a grindstone and a lathe, each with fast-and-loose pulleys and a belt shifter. Run: engage belts one by one; shaft and machine rpm from pulley ratios.
