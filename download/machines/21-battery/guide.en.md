## What it does

Copper, zinc and an electrolyte make the first steady electric current.

This part of the tree: A battery, then a magnet, then a wheel-driven alternator. From here power can travel down a wire.

## Model

A row of Daniell cells in glass jars (copper pot in copper sulfate, zinc in a porous clay pot), wired in series to a galvanometer/voltmeter and a small lamp or buzzer, plus a Volta pile beside it for history. Run: add cells one by one; volts step up 1.1 V per cell; the lamp lights (lit).
