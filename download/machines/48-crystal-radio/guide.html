  • Key number: no battery
  • Needs: 11. Wire draw plate
  • Unlocks: 49. Spark-gap transmitter

    • What it does

    Hears broadcasts with no power at all: a coil, a crystal and an earphone.

    This part of the tree: Print, time, direction, then messages over wires and through the air, and finally a machine that calculates.

    Model

    A crystal set on a wooden board: a long-wire antenna to a pole, an earth stake, a tuning coil on a tube with a sliding contact, a cat's-whisker detector on galena (or a foxhole razor-blade detector), a high-impedance earphone. Run: slide the tuner; signal strength peaks on a station; no battery.
