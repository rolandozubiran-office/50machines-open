## What it does

A generator run backwards: pumps, fans and power tools anywhere.

This part of the tree: A battery, then a magnet, then a wheel-driven alternator. From here power can travel down a wire.

## Model

A hand-built DC motor: a wound armature with a commutator, carbon brushes, field magnets or field coils on a cast frame, bearings, a pulley driving a small pump or fan, fed from a battery through a switch. Run: voltage slider → rpm; reverse leads to show direction.
