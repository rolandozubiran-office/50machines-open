## What it does

Burnt limestone gives mortar, whitewash and a disinfectant.

This part of the tree: Fired clay, boiled water and soap stop most early deaths. Charcoal and lime set up the heat and materials for everything after.

## Model

A vertical shaft lime kiln of stone against a bank, with alternating layers of limestone and fuel visible in cutaway, a draw arch at the bottom, and a slaking pit with water beside it. Run: burn above 900 °C, draw quicklime, slake it (steam, heat), readout 1.75 kg limestone per kg of lime.
