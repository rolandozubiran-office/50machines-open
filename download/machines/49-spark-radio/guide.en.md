## What it does

Sends Morse by radio. Marconi crossed the Atlantic with one.

This part of the tree: Print, time, direction, then messages over wires and through the air, and finally a machine that calculates.

## Model

A spark transmitter: battery, induction coil with interrupter, Morse key, spark gap with brass balls, an antenna and an earth plate; across the room a coherer receiver ringing a bell. Run: key a letter; sparks (lit) jump, the coherer trips the bell. Legal/safety line: spark transmitters jam radio and are illegal to operate in most countries.
