## What it does

The Icyball: heat it on a flame, then it makes cold all day. No electricity.

This part of the tree: Cold food, hot water, cooking gas and engines that run on wood or sunlight.

## Model

A Crosley Icyball: two welded steel balls joined by a tube (hot ball with ammonia-water, cold ball with fins), a stand with a kerosene burner for charging, and an insulated icebox the cold ball sits in. Run: phase 1 heat ~1.5 h (sped), pressure to ~249 psi, ammonia boils over to the cold ball; phase 2 move to the icebox, cold ball frosts (lit), icebox temperature falls, ~24 h of cooling. Strong pressure/ammonia safety line.
