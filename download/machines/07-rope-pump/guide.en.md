## What it does

A loop of rope and washers lifts water with a hand crank.

This part of the tree: Fired clay, boiled water and soap stop most early deaths. Charcoal and lime set up the heat and materials for everything after.

## Model

A rope pump over a well: a frame with a crank wheel, a rope loop with pistons (washers) running down a riser pipe into the well (cutaway shaft with water), returning through a guide at the bottom; water spills from an outlet spout into a trough. Run: crank speed slider → litres per minute, ~40 L/min from 10 m (Akvopedia, flagged weak in notes).
