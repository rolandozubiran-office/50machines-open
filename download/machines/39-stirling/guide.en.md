## What it does

An engine run by any outside heat, from a fire to a mirror in the sun.

This part of the tree: Cold food, hot water, cooking gas and engines that run on wood or sunlight.

## Model

A gamma Stirling engine over a burner: a hot displacer cylinder with fins on the cold end, a power cylinder, connecting rods to a crank and a heavy flywheel, driving a small belt load. Run: heat slider → temperature difference → rpm and watts.
