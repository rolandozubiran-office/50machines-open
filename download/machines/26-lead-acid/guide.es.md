## Modelo

Nota de modelo, en inglés, tal como está en `data/machines.mjs`:

Planté cells: spiral-rolled lead sheets with separators in glass jars of ~10% sulfuric acid, six in series to 12 V, terminals, a hydrometer. Run: forming by repeated charge reversal, then charge (bubbles) and discharge into a lamp; 2 V per cell. Strong acid safety line.
