## What it does

Air-blown charcoal welds and shapes iron into every tool that follows.

This part of the tree: A forge, a foundry and a lathe make tools that make tools. Water wheels and gears put a river to work.

## Model

A brick forge hearth with a tuyere fed by a great double-action bellows on a lever, an anvil on a stump, a quench tub, tongs and a hammer on a rack. Run: pump the bellows; the fire climbs through colour heats to forge-welding (1,400–1,500 °C); the bar glows (lit) and is hammered.
