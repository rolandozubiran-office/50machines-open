## What it does

Remelts scrap aluminium and bronze into cast pulleys, gears and frames.

This part of the tree: A forge, a foundry and a lathe make tools that make tools. Water wheels and gears put a river to work.

## Model

A Gingery-style charcoal foundry: a lined bucket furnace with a blower, a crucible, lifting tongs, sand flasks (cope and drag) with a pulley pattern, and ingot moulds. Run: melt scrap aluminium (≈ 660 °C), skim, pour into the mould, open the flask to reveal a cast pulley.
