## What it does

Boiling kills waterborne germs; a rocket stove does it on 40–50% less wood than an open fire.

This part of the tree: Fired clay, boiled water and soap stop most early deaths. Charcoal and lime set up the heat and materials for everything after.

## Model

An L-shaped rocket stove of fired clay/brick: fuel magazine, insulated combustion chamber and chimney riser, pot skirt, a pot of water on it, and a lidded clay storage jar with a tap beside it. Run: feed sticks; water heats to 100 °C, a 1-minute rolling boil (CDC), then it is poured into the jar; readout compares wood used with a three-stone fire.
