## What it does

Sand and a living biofilm clean drinking water with no fuel at all.

This part of the tree: Fired clay, boiled water and soap stop most early deaths. Charcoal and lime set up the heat and materials for everything after.

## Model

A concrete biosand filter shown in cutaway: lid, diffuser plate, ~5 cm standing water over the biolayer, fine sand, coarse sand and gravel, the outlet tube rising to set the water level, a bucket under the spout, and a safe-storage container. Run: pour a 20 L batch; water seeps down and flows out of the spout at a realistic rate; readout gives litres and the field log reductions from the notes.
