  • Needs: None. It does not depend on an earlier machine.
  • Unlocks: 2. Rocket stove and boiler; 3. Ash-lye soap kettle; 4. Biosand water filter; 5. Charcoal kiln; 6. Lime kiln; 8. Glass furnace; 10. Scrap metal foundry; 33. Zeer pot cooler; 40. Biogas digester

    • What it does

    Fired clay makes pots, filters, crucibles and the firebrick every later furnace needs.

    This part of the tree: Fired clay, boiled water and soap stop most early deaths. Charcoal and lime set up the heat and materials for everything after.

    Model

    An updraft kiln: a brick firebox with arches under a perforated floor, green pots and bricks stacked inside, a domed chamber and a chimney, with a spy hole and pyrometric cones. Run: stoke the fire through a sped-up firing; temperature climbs to ~1,000 °C, the chamber glow (lit) deepens, cones bend at their temperatures, then a slow cool.
