## What it does

The first machine tool: turns axles, handles and bearings round.

This part of the tree: A forge, a foundry and a lathe make tools that make tools. Water wheels and gears put a river to work.

## Model

A spring-pole lathe: timber bed, two poppets with centres, a tool rest, a treadle, and a cord running from the treadle round the workpiece up to a springy pole overhead. Run: treadle down spins the work toward the chisel, the pole springs it back; a chair leg takes shape.
