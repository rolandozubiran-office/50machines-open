    • What it does

    Joins steel with current: tanks, frames and pressure vessels.

    This part of the tree: A battery, then a magnet, then a wheel-driven alternator. From here power can travel down a wire.

    Model

    A battery arc welder: three 12 V batteries in series with a heavy resistance/control coil, welding cables, an electrode holder, an earth clamp on a steel workbench, a mask. Run: strike the arc (lit glow), a bead runs along the joint. Safety: UV, mask, ventilation, hydrogen from batteries.
