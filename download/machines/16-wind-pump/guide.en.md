## What it does

The multi-blade windmill pumps water with no fuel at all.

This part of the tree: A forge, a foundry and a lathe make tools that make tools. Water wheels and gears put a river to work.

## Model

A multi-blade farm windmill on a lattice tower with a tail vane, a crank and a long pump rod down to a piston pump at a borehole, discharging into a storage tank. Run: wind speed slider → rotor rpm → strokes → m³/day; Practical Action sizing kWh/day = 0.002725 × m³/day × head.
