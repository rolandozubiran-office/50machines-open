## What it does

A pendulum cut clock error from about 15 minutes a day to 15 seconds.

This part of the tree: Print, time, direction, then messages over wires and through the air, and finally a machine that calculates.

## Model

A weight-driven pendulum clock movement in an open frame: barrel and weight on a cord, gear train, anchor escapement and escape wheel, a ~1 m seconds pendulum (T = 2π√(L/g)), and a dial with hands. Run: release the weight; escapement ticks, hands move (sped time), accuracy readout.
