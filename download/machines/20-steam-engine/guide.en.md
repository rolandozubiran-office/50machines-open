## What it does

Turns burning wood or coal into motion anywhere, not just beside a river.

This part of the tree: A forge, a foundry and a lathe make tools that make tools. Water wheels and gears put a river to work.

## Model

A vertical fire-tube boiler with a firebox, safety valve, pressure gauge and sight glass, piped to a single-cylinder double-acting engine with a crosshead, connecting rod, crank, flywheel and a flyball governor. Run: fire up, pressure rises, open the throttle, engine runs; rpm and hp. Strong boiler safety line.
