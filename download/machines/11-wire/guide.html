11. Wire draw plate

Card generated from the open catalog in this repository. It records the machine's place in the tree, its key number and the model note already published there. It does not add build steps or numbers that are not in the catalog.

  • Part: II · Metal and motion
  • Slug: wire

  • Key number: anneal every ~3 passes
  • Needs: 9. Bellows and forge
  • Unlocks: 21. Voltaic battery; 22. Electromagnet; 46. Telegraph; 48. Crystal radio

    • What it does

    Pulls copper rod through ever smaller holes into wire for coils and cables.

    This part of the tree: A forge, a foundry and a lathe make tools that make tools. Water wheels and gears put a river to work.

    Model

    A draw bench: a steel draw plate with a row of holes clamped upright, tongs on a chain to a winch/capstan, a coil of copper rod, an annealing fire, and a spool of finished wire. Run: pull through successive holes, gauge shrinks, anneal every ~3 passes.
