## What it does

A carbon filament in a vacuum. Carbonised bamboo lasted over 1,200 hours.

This part of the tree: A battery, then a magnet, then a wheel-driven alternator. From here power can travel down a wire.

## Model

A lamp workshop: carbonising furnace for bamboo filaments, a glass bulb with lead-in wires on a stem, and a hand vacuum pump connected to the bulb, then the sealed bulb in a socket on a battery. Run: pump down, seal, switch on; the filament glows (lit) as current rises.
