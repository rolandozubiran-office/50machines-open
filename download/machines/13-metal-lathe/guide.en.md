## What it does

A lathe that helps build itself, then the shaper, mill and drill press.

This part of the tree: A forge, a foundry and a lathe make tools that make tools. Water wheels and gears put a river to work.

## Model

A Gingery-style metal lathe: cast aluminium bed with flat ways, headstock with step pulleys and a belt from a motor, carriage with cross-slide and toolpost, leadscrew, tailstock. Run: turning a steel shaft, the carriage traverses, a diameter readout drops pass by pass.
