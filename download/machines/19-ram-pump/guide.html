    • What it does

    Uses the water's own momentum to pump uphill, day and night, with no fuel.

    This part of the tree: A forge, a foundry and a lathe make tools that make tools. Water wheels and gears put a river to work.

    Model

    A hydraulic ram: supply tank with a drive pipe down to the ram body (waste valve, check valve, air chamber), delivery pipe up to a high tank on a hill. Run: the waste valve clacks 30–100 times a minute; water climbs to the high tank; delivery per day from the efficiency relation (state assumptions).
