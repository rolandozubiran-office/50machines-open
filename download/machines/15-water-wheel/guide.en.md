## What it does

An overshot wheel keeps most of the water's power: the first big engine.

This part of the tree: A forge, a foundry and a lathe make tools that make tools. Water wheels and gears put a river to work.

## Model

An overshot water wheel whose axle carries cams that lift a tilt (trip) hammer over an anvil, a forge-mill. Run: flow slider → wheel rpm → hammer blows per minute, power from 9.81·Q·H·η.
