    • What it does

    Salvage panels first; a home-made selenium or copper-oxide cell proves the effect.

    This part of the tree: A battery, then a magnet, then a wheel-driven alternator. From here power can travel down a wire.

    Model

    Two salvaged PV panels on a tilting rack with a charge controller and battery, and beside them a home-made cuprous-oxide cell (oxidised copper sheet and plain copper in salt water in a jar) on a microammeter. Run: sun angle / time-of-day slider → panel watts and the homemade cell's tiny current.
