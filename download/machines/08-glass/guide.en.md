## What it does

Glass for jars, lenses, light bulbs and sight glasses.

This part of the tree: Fired clay, boiled water and soap stop most early deaths. Charcoal and lime set up the heat and materials for everything after.

## Model

A small beehive glass furnace with a glory hole and a crucible, a marver table, blowpipe, and an annealing oven. Run: melt the batch (74% silica, 16% soda, 10% lime) to ~1,320 °C, gather at ~1,090 °C, blow a jar, anneal at 371–482 °C.
