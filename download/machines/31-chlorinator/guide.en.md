## What it does

Current through brine makes chlorine to disinfect drinking water.

This part of the tree: A battery, then a magnet, then a wheel-driven alternator. From here power can travel down a wire.

## Model

A WATA-style electrochlorinator: a container of brine with an electrode cell hung in it, powered by a 12 V battery or small solar panel, a timer, and dosing into jerrycans of water. Run: 2-hour production cycle sped up; concentration rises to ~6 g/L; dosing. Safety: chlorine gas, ventilate, label.
