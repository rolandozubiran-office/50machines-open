## What it does

A crossflow turbine copes with changing flow better than a wheel.

This part of the tree: A battery, then a magnet, then a wheel-driven alternator. From here power can travel down a wire.

## Model

A crossflow (Michell-Banki) turbine: intake with screen, a penstock pipe down a slope, the turbine housing with guide vane and drum runner, a belt to a generator, a control panel with a ballast load. Run: flow and guide-vane slider → kW from 9.81·Q·H·η (70–85%).
