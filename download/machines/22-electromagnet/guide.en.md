## What it does

Wire wound on iron: the heart of motors, relays, telegraphs and dynamos.

This part of the tree: A battery, then a magnet, then a wheel-driven alternator. From here power can travel down a wire.

## Model

A horseshoe electromagnet: soft-iron core with many turns of wire, a knife switch, battery, an armature bar hung from a spring balance or a crane-like stand lifting scrap iron. Run: close the switch; current flows (lit), the armature snaps up and lifts load; turns × current.
