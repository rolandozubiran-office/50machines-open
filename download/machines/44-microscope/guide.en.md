## What it does

A single glass bead magnifies enough to see microbes, the start of germ theory.

This part of the tree: Print, time, direction, then messages over wires and through the air, and finally a machine that calculates.

## Model

A Leeuwenhoek-style single-lens microscope: two riveted brass plates holding a tiny glass bead lens, a specimen pin on a focusing screw and a height screw; plus the bead-making set-up (a glass rod in a flame). Run: focus and magnification; show the view as an inset circle on a white card in the figure.
