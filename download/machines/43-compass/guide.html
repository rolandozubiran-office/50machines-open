    • What it does

    A magnetised needle on a silk thread points north.

    This part of the tree: Print, time, direction, then messages over wires and through the air, and finally a machine that calculates.

    Model

    A navigator's set: a lodestone, a steel needle stroked to magnetise it, a wooden compass box with a pivoted card or a needle on a float in a water bowl, a sundial-style baseplate with a sighting vane. Run: rotate the box; the needle swings and settles north; bearing readout.
