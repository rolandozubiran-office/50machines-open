    This part of the tree: A forge, a foundry and a lathe make tools that make tools. Water wheels and gears put a river to work.

    A water mill's gear train in cutaway: wheel shaft with a pit wheel, wallower, great spur wheel, stone nut, and a pair of millstones in a wooden tun with hopper, shoe and meal spout into a sack. Run: open the water; gears turn, grain feeds, flour fills the sack; kg/h vs a hand quern.
