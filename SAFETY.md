# Safety and moderation rules

1. **Refused outright:** weapons, explosives, firearms parts, pathogens, drugs, surveillance tools, and anything whose main use is harm.
2. **Allowed with a second reviewer and a clear safety line:** fire and kilns, molten metal, pressure vessels and steam, ammonia, chlorine, carbon monoxide (wood gas), lead, acids and alkalis, high current, radio transmitters (illegal to operate unlicensed in most countries).
3. Every hazardous step has its hazard in the `check` or `lead`, and `meta.json` → `safety` has one plain line.
4. No medical claims beyond published sources. No legal advice.
5. Guides explain how parts go together. They are not engineering drawings and do not certify anything.
6. Maintainers can remove any community machine at any time if a problem is found.
